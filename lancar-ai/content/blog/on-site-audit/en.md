"Audit" sounds like an inspection. Ours is closer to a day of careful watching: we want to see how work moves through your business, where it waits, and where people do by hand what a tool could do for them.

## Before the visit

We start with a free call to understand your business and agree on the scope. We sign a confidentiality agreement, and we ask you to tell your team why we are coming. People speak more freely once they know that nobody is being evaluated.

## During the visit

We follow the path of a customer, from the first message to the last payment.

- **At the front desk or on the sales line**, we watch how enquiries arrive and get answered: which channel, how fast, in which language, and which questions keep coming back.
- **With the person who keeps the records**, we look at where bookings, sales and invoices are written down, and how many times the same information is typed.
- **In the back office**, we look at stock, suppliers, schedules and the reports the owner receives.
- **With the owner or manager**, we ask what they check every day, what worries them, and what they would do with ten more hours a week.

We ask to see real examples: an actual chat, an actual invoice, the actual spreadsheet. The gap between how a process is described and how it runs is where most of the opportunities are.

## After the visit

Within seven working days you receive a written report, in Indonesian or English, with four parts:

1. **A map of your main processes** as they really run.
2. **A ranked list of opportunities.** For each one: the hours it could save, what it costs to set up, what it costs to run each month, and what could go wrong.
3. **Quick wins** your team can apply straight away, for free. Often these are habits and templates, not AI.
4. **A fixed-price quote** for the solutions worth building.

The report is yours. You can implement it with us, with someone else, or on your own.

## Three things you can prepare

You do not need to prepare anything, but these three make the day more useful:

- **The ten questions customers ask most.** Your front desk can list them from memory.
- **A count of messages over one normal day,** by channel.
- **A list of every file and app the business runs on,** including the notebook in the drawer.

Even if you never book an audit, writing these three lists will tell you a lot about where your hours go.
