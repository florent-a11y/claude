When someone offers you "a chatbot for your WhatsApp", they may mean one of three very different things. Knowing the difference saves money in both directions: you avoid paying for more than you need, and you avoid a cheap tool that annoys your customers.

## Level 1: automatic replies

A fixed message sent in a fixed situation: a greeting to a new contact, an away message outside opening hours, saved quick replies your staff can send with two taps.

These come free with the WhatsApp Business app, and they take ten minutes to set up. WhatsApp has also begun offering a built-in AI agent for small businesses. If it is available on your account, try it: for simple questions it may be all you need.

**Good for:** telling people when you will reply, and sharing basic information.

**The limit:** the message is the same whatever the customer asked.

## Level 2: the menu chatbot

"Press 1 for prices, 2 for location, 3 to talk to our staff." The customer follows a path that somebody designed in advance.

**Good for:** a small number of predictable requests, such as tracking an order.

**The limit:** people do not write in menus. A question that does not fit a button leads to a dead end, and every language has to be written separately.

## Level 3: the AI assistant

The customer writes freely, in any language, and the assistant understands the question. It answers from information you have approved. Connected to your calendar or stock list, it can also act: check availability, create a booking, prepare a quote. When it is unsure, it hands the conversation to a person.

**Good for:** varied questions, several languages, and requests that depend on live information.

**The limit:** it needs accurate information, a connection to your systems and some supervision in the first weeks. It costs more to set up than the first two.

## Five questions to choose

1. **How many messages a day?** Under ten, your team can probably answer by hand with good quick replies.
2. **How varied are they?** If five questions cover almost everything, a simple tool goes a long way.
3. **How many languages?** Beyond two, an AI assistant becomes hard to replace.
4. **Does the answer depend on live data?** "Is Saturday at 4 pm free?" cannot be answered by a fixed message.
5. **What is a missed message worth?** One lost villa booking can cost more than a month of any tool.

## Our advice

Start with level 1 today: it is free, and it will show you which questions come up most. Move up when the limits start costing you bookings, and take the list of those questions with you. It is the best brief you can give whoever sets up the next level.
